## LinkedIn
Every Indian has a "which office do I even go to?" story: a pothole nobody fixes, a pension that stopped, a UPI scam.

The laws to fix these exist. The RTI Act says a department must reply within 30 days. RBI rules give you zero liability for a fraud reported within 3 days. Almost nobody knows how to use them.

At the Cline AI Builder Hackathon at IIT Guwahati, we built **Haq (हक़)**, an AI caseworker:
🎙️ Speak your problem in Hindi, Assamese or any Indian language, and add a photo
🏛️ It finds the office actually responsible (GMC vs PWD vs NHAI) from a grounded directory, not hallucinations
📝 It drafts a formal complaint and an RTI application, then runs a filing checklist and fixes its own mistakes (no "why" questions, under 500 words, correct sections)
⏰ It tracks the statutory deadlines and writes your First Appeal when the department goes silent

Under the hood, every case is a @Cline SDK agent with custom tools that works through a case folder the way Cline works through a repo: plan → act → verify. We also used Cline as our coding partner for the whole build.
